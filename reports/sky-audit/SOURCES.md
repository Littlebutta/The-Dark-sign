# DS3 reference pictures for the sky audit (downloaded 2026-11-24)

Saved byte for byte as each site served them: no re-encoding or cropping. Fandom images were fetched with `&format=original`, because without it the server sends a WebP copy under a .jpg name. All images are Dark Souls III screenshots (game (c) FromSoftware / Bandai Namco). They are kept here for internal comparison only and must not be redistributed with the mod.

Sources:
- **Dark Souls Wiki (Fandom)**, https://darksouls.fandom.com: the "Area - NN" screenshot series. The uploader comes from each file page (MediaWiki API `imageinfo.user`).
- **Fextralife Dark Souls 3 Wiki**, https://darksouls3.wiki.fextralife.com: images on the area pages. No per-image author is shown there.

Notes for the comparison:
- Many DS3 skies **change with story progress**. After the Lords of Cinder fall, High Wall, Undead Settlement, Lothric Castle, Grand Archives, Cathedral and Kiln get an orange sky with the **eclipse (Darksign)**. Several references show that state, e.g. 02-...-07, 03-...-06, 05, 09-...-06, 10-*. Compare against the pre-eclipse ones where both exist.
- **Archdragon Peak is a bright blue sky with white clouds in DS3 too** (11-*). The blue sky seen in game there is broadly right.
- The Ringed City reference shows a yellow-white cloud sea under a hazy sun (15-*), not blue.
- Painted World (13-*) and Dreg Heap (14-*) only have 512x256 loading-screen-sized shots on Fandom; Fextralife's Ariandel images are 768x432.
- Irithyll Dungeon / Profaned Capital (08-*) has no clear outdoor sky shot on either site; the Profaned Capital pictures show the area under a dark sky.
